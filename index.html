01 / OVERVIEW

Built for the
modern battlespace.

USS Arleigh Burke (DDG-51) is the lead ship of the Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyers.

Named after Admiral Arleigh Burke, the ship represents a major class of U.S. Navy surface combatants designed around the Aegis combat system.

The class combines air-defense capabilities, surface warfare, anti-submarine warfare and other missions within a single multi-role platform.
